DEC: in between

Say how much each theory counts

The provenance verb puts each theory in one of three worlds, and reasoning follows it:

Doxasticbelieve: a belief, kept apart from the facts Epistemicaccept: knowledge, it becomes fact Conjecturalsuppose: a hypothesis built on the facts Delusionaldetected: a theory that contradicts the facts

Theories coexist, and clashes are detected.
